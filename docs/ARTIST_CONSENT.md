# Separate artist consent

A recovery proposal can shorten the headliner set. The producer's ordinary roles cannot authorize that artist commitment. `/approvals` now lets the original producer designate a representative for one exact proposal; `/approvals/review` gives that invitee a dedicated review capability. The producer makes a separate final plan decision after the current consent is saved.

The invitation records the candidate and source digests, original production revision, requirement, named representative, stated basis, and exact before/after artist changes. Its fixed expiry is the earliest of the requested 5–60 minute window, current quote expiry, and original producer session expiry. The app never extends it. A changed proposal or ended/revoked review needs a new current proposal and new review.

The reviewer capability does not create an `Actor` or generic session. It reads only that review and records one explicitly declared approve/decline decision. The producer's original session token fingerprint and full microsecond expiry remain part of every new instruction. A same-actor replacement token, changed expiry, another tenant, crew access, and a producer pretending to hold artist authority are refused. Declared identity is not independently verified; controlled rehearsal clients are labeled as controlled declarations and establish no real human participation.

Requests, decisions, command receipts, revocations, and consumptions use five additive immutable tables in migration 19. Private review/capability/decision payloads are encrypted with tenant- and purpose-bound context; decision integrity is checked with a separate HMAC. The final producer approval consumes the verified current decision under the existing production transaction and retains a safe proof in the approved plan. Original decisions remain historical after later source changes, expiry or access revocation. Ordinary plan approval still cannot borrow this proof implicitly.

Both clients retain exact instruction bytes before sending. An uncertain response is recovered by original command GET; a verified missing receipt permits an explicit retry with the same identity and bytes. Receipt reads serialize behind a current production transaction. Changed access, corrupted journals, unreadable receipts and mismatched body hashes retain the unresolved instruction. They do not create another command. No message or email is sent by this feature; the producer can copy the private review link for the intended recipient.

The review itself neither pays nor publishes, records physical work, creates crew readback, nor issues GO. Supplier availability, current signed quote, native graph result, resource/policy constraints and existing publication/commercial gates remain independent. A statement with unresolved conditions is not treated as evidence that those conditions were met.

Local qualification uses a new restricted database and role, actual signed local supplier RFQs and actual licensed Bryntum engine commits. Its positive declarations are explicitly controlled. Separate tests cover concurrent replay, original expiry after a lock wait, signature/digest corruption, source drift, strict APIs, body/hash recovery, mounted client behavior and preservation of existing rows during migration. Earlier setup/type/test failures remain in the private qualification record. Native browser and compiled runtime qualification are reported separately; this document makes no human, live model, financial provider or physical sign-off claim.

The existing actual-model P02 result remains a correctly refused historical proposal. This new workflow does not rewrite that response or retroactively qualify its expired original quote.
